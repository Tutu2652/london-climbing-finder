Westway Climbing Centre

London's biggest climbing wall, climbing since 1994 at the Westway Sports & Fitness Centre (run by Everyone Active): 350 routes on 110 rope lines up to 13.5 m, 450 m² of bouldering reset every month, auto-belays and a serious training room. A family-friendly, all-ages centre known for its NICAS and junior coaching.
