The Nest

An independent, community-built gym started by two climbing friends and opened in 2019 after a crowdfunding push, to bring climbing to Hillingdon. More community hub than climbing wall: bouldering, a gym, yoga and a café under one roof, with women's socials, board-game nights, a running club and lots of youth and school work. Around 300 members.

Bouldering in Hayes (The Old Vinyl Factory), zone 5. 4 m bouldering wall with about 120 problems VB-V8+ (vertical, slab, overhang, cave, traverse), a quarter reset every week; kids' climbing; 3,000 sq ft Technogym gym and free weights; 50+ fitness and yoga classes a month included with membership; café with viewing area and free Wi-Fi; free on-site parking. Shoe hire £2.50, chalk £2.50
