Mount Palace

One of London's oldest, smallest and friendliest climbing centres: a not-for-profit micro gym run by Crystal Palace locals, with roots in a climbing scene going back to 1991 and the Southern Sandstone crags nearby. It keeps prices low on purpose, works with local charities, and every staff member is a qualified roped-climbing instructor. Boulders use Font grades set to match outdoor climbing, so expect them a little stiff.

Ropes And Bouldering in Crystal Palace (National Sports Centre), zone 3. Bouldering F3+ to F7A plus two ungraded traverses; four top-rope bays (free harness & rope hire); textured feature wall mimicking Southern Sandstone; training area with 4 m campus board, Beastmaker, strap trainer and weights; topos by the wall; free shoe & chalk hire; showers, changing rooms and lockers (bring a padlock) in the National Sports Centre; free parking. Open in sessions only, closed Fri 3:30-6pm; last entry 1 hour before close
