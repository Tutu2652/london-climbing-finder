Yonder Climbing

Independent and rooted in Walthamstow, Yonder began in 2016 as a Kickstarter project: London's first climbing, yoga and workspace under one roof ("between a rock and a workspace"). It mixes climbing, coworking and wellbeing into one community, tries to learn names and faces, and runs an access-to-climbing scheme to bring in more people.
