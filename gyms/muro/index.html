Muro Climbing Peckham

An independent, community-focused bouldering wall in a Peckham railway arch by Copeland Park. Deliberately intimate, it aims to be a home-from-home for Peckham's diverse local community, with an open, playful atmosphere for climbers of every level.

Bouldering in Peckham Rye, zone 2. High-density bouldering wall, competition-height bouldering wall, shoe & chalk hire £4
