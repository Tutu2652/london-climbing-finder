Climbing District - Bermondsey

Climbing District's Building One, billed as London's original bouldering hub: a 15,000 sq ft centre in a former biscuit factory hall a few minutes from Bermondsey tube, and one of the city's largest climbing floors. Steep angles and punchy setting for climbers who like to climb hard, alongside regular resets and workshops for beginners.

Bouldering in Bermondsey, zone 2. Bouldering, training area, MoonBoard, spray wall, large traverse walls · AC in training room/slab wall
