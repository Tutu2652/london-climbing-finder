The Font Wandsworth

The Font's newest site, in Southside Shopping Centre on Garratt Lane, and the only one with ropes: hundreds of boulders plus auto-belay and roped walls up to 12 m, all regularly reset. There's a large functional fitness space (free weights, sled, squat and bench stations, calisthenics rig) and a café-bar with a workspace.
