Substation Brixton

A bouldering and fitness centre in Brixton (with a sister site in Macclesfield) that uses its own grading system: start and top tags with levels 1–6 instead of colour grades, so routes mix hold colours and climbers spread across the wall. Alongside the climbing there's a fitness studio, a wild sauna and plunge, and a café, pizzeria and workspace.

Bouldering in Brixton Hill, zone 2. Bouldering, fitness gym, studio classes, sauna/cold plunge, café, showers/lockers, free parking
