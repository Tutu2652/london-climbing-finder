Yellow Spider Climbing

One of five Spider Climbing centres and strongly family-focused: kids' sessions, holiday climbing, termly clubs, birthday parties and adult-plus-child memberships, alongside adult socials and coaching.

Bouldering in Carshalton (Oaks Sports Centre), zone 6. Two bouldering rooms (Comp Room and Rob's Room) reset every two weeks on alternating Fridays; easier areas for beginners and harder ones for experienced climbers; arêtes, laybacks and plenty of volumes; shoe hire £3.90, chalk bag £2, free helmet
