The Font Hounslow

Formerly Hang, now part of The Font: a 5,400 sq ft bouldering gym packed with varied angles, plus the latest training boards and a fitness gym with fingerboards. The workspace and lounge sit on a raised level with views over the centre. Opposite Asda on Smithy Lane, near Hounslow East and Central, and 8 minutes from Heathrow Terminals 2 & 3.
