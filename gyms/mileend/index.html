Mile End Climbing Wall

Run by the charity Develop through Challenge, in an old pipe engineering works in Mile End Park, open since the late 1980s. A big, relaxed and informal wall (about 1,500 m² of climbing) used by charities, schools and clubs as much as regulars, with the Board Room and the famous Monkey House for training. Proud to be one of London's most affordable walls; your entry supports the charity.
