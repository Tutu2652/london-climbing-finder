Rhino Boulder Bromley

Bromley's climbing hub, built for everyone from first-timers to strong climbers, with quick links to central London and Kent. Climbing, coworking desks and a café in one place, a big youth community, and Climb & Connect: free climbing for referred Bromley residents, funded by the council and the NHS.

Bouldering in Bromley town centre, zone 5. Bouldering only, routes graded by tag colour and reset regularly; dedicated training area; stretch & warm-up area; yoga & Pilates; coworking space with extended hours; café & shop; changing rooms & showers; private lockers; dust extraction; shoe hire £4.50 (free for members), chalk £1. Under-14s climb Mon-Fri until 7pm, weekends until 5pm
